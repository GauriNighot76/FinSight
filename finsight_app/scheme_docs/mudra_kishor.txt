Mudra Loan - Kishor category

What it is: Part of the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY), a government scheme offering small collateral-free loans to micro businesses, split into three tiers based on loan amount: Shishu (up to Rs 50,000), Kishor (Rs 50,001 to Rs 5 lakh), and Tarun (Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh). This document covers the Kishor tier specifically, meant for businesses with some existing track record, not brand-new startups.

Who qualifies: Non-corporate, non-farm micro and small business owners, typically aged 18 to 65, whose funding need falls in the Rs 50,001 to Rs 5 lakh range. Applicable to manufacturing, trading, and service sector businesses.

Benefit: Collateral-free loan between Rs 50,001 and Rs 5 lakh, offered through banks, NBFCs, and microfinance institutions participating in the MUDRA scheme.

How to apply: Applications can be made online through the official Udyamimitra portal, or directly at a participating bank branch. Required documents typically include identity proof, address proof, business existence proof (like a shop registration or Udyam certificate), and a simple project/business plan describing how the loan will be used.

Source: PM Mudra Yojana eligibility documentation, HDFC Bank scheme guide.
