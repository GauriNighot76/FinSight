PMEGP - Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (Service/Business category)

What it is: A government subsidy scheme run by KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission) that helps new micro-enterprises get started by covering part of the project cost through a bank loan with a government subsidy attached.

Who qualifies: Individuals aged 18 or above starting a NEW service or business unit (not an expansion of an existing unit). There is no income ceiling. Maximum project cost for service/business category is Rs 20 lakh.

Important: applicants who have already received a subsidy under any other central or state government scheme for the same or a related purpose are not eligible. This is a "new unit" scheme, not meant for businesses that already received government financial assistance before.

Benefit: Government subsidy of 15% to 35% of the project cost, depending on category of applicant and location (urban/rural) and general/special category status. The remaining project cost is financed as a bank loan.

How to apply: Applications are submitted online through the official PMEGP e-portal. Required documents typically include: Aadhaar card, educational qualification proof, project report/business plan, caste certificate (if applicable for subsidy category), and bank account details. After online submission, the application is reviewed by the District Industries Centre or KVIC, followed by bank loan sanction.

Source: KVIC PMEGP e-portal, eligibility criteria page.
